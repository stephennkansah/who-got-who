Who Got Who Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Who Got Who uses only the information needed to run a temporary multiplayer game.

Information we process

  • Your chosen player name and optional avatar.
  • Game membership, tasks, claims, votes, scores, and challenge completions.
  • Optional photo proof for challenges that clearly request it.
  • A pseudonymous AWS Cognito device identity, plus normal security logs such as IP address and browser information.

Secret pending tasks are returned only to the device identity that owns them. Photo uploads are private and are not used for advertising or model training by us. Ask permission from every identifiable person before uploading a photo.

Storage and retention

Amazon Web Services provides Cognito, AppSync, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, and Amplify Hosting. Ended games expire after about 24 hours; inactive games expire after about 7 days. Uploaded avatars and proof images are automatically removed within 30 days. Deletion may complete shortly after these periods because cloud cleanup is asynchronous.

Your choices

Photo avatars are optional. Do not upload sensitive information. You may leave a game at any time. To request access or early deletion, email info@myformatic.com with the game code and approximate creation time.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information.
