Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. Introduction

Who Got Who ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Who Got Who mobile application and website (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use Who Got Who, you may provide:

  • Player names (used only within game sessions)
  • Game data (tasks, scores, game history)
  • Optional profile information (avatars, preferences)
  • Optional photo proof for challenges that clearly request it

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use our Service:

  • Device information (device type, operating system)
  • A pseudonymous device identity used to reconnect you to your game
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining the Service
  • Enabling multiplayer game functionality
  • Improving and optimizing the Service
  • Ensuring security and preventing fraud

4. Data Storage

Game data is stored with Amazon Web Services (AWS). Ended games expire after about 24 hours and inactive games expire after about 7 days. Uploaded avatars and photo proof are private objects and are automatically removed within 30 days. AWS deletion may be asynchronous, so removal can occur shortly after those periods.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Amazon Web Services - Cognito, AppSync, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, and Amplify Hosting provide identity, multiplayer, storage, and hosting services (AWS Privacy Notice)

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of data collection (though this may limit Service functionality)

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
